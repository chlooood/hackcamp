# Desk research (public sources only)

We did not interview anyone. Say so in the pitch. Every line below is from a public source found on 2026-11-07. Re-open the link before quoting a number on stage. Secondary sources are marked.

## The outage problem in BC
- A past December windstorm left 750,000 BC Hydro customers without power. Tens of thousands waited more than three days. BC Hydro called it the largest outage in its history. Source (secondary, news magazine quoting BC Hydro): https://www.darpanmagazine.com/news/national/british-columbians-unprepared-for-increasingly-severe-weather-bc-hydro/
- BC Hydro survey of 800 customers: more than 60 percent had not taken steps to prepare for storm outages, and only half had an emergency kit. Same source as above. Confirm on bchydro.com before using.
- BC Hydro's own preparation advice (as reproduced in First Nation notices) tells people to connect with their neighbours and, if they rely on life-sustaining equipment and the outage is long, to move to the home of a family member or friend in an area with power. Sources: https://www.seabirdisland.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Planned-Power-Outage_converted.pdf and https://tlaztennation.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Power-outage-notice_Tlazten_20250323.pdf

## Health risk
- US Medicare study (PLOS, 2026): outages lasting longer than 8 hours were associated with more hospitalizations for cardiovascular and respiratory disease among adults over 65. Estimated 4,246 extra hospitalizations in 2018. US data, not BC. Source: https://e3.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1118555
- Narrative review of power outages and health, including electricity-dependent medical equipment: https://springerlink.bibliotecabuap.elogim.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s40572-020-00295-0.pdf (mirror copy, find the original Current Environmental Health Reports article before citing)

## Registries are not enough
- NYC survey: among respondents with electric-dependent household members, only 40 percent reported being registered with a utility for early outage notification. Small subgroup, New York, not BC. Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11524-018-0296-9
- Even registered life-support customers are told to keep their own backup plan. Source (Australian regulator fact sheet): https://aer.gov.au/system/files/2024-05/Energy%20and%20essential%20medical%20equipment%20fact%20sheet.pdf
- A Louisiana pilot (Power Outage Partners) proactively reaches people on ventilators. It shows the problem is recognised and that the answer today is programs, not a daily-use app. Source: https://www.astho.org/globalassets/report/advancing-preparedness-for-life-support.pdf

## Isolation
- National Institute on Ageing, Ageing in Canada survey: 43 percent of Canadians aged 50 and over are at high risk of social isolation and 57 percent report feeling lonely. Source: https://niageing.ca/resources/social-isolation-and-lonelinessthe-hidden-epidemic/
- Isolation and loneliness are not the same thing. Some people are comfortable alone. Do not assume everyone who lives alone wants contact. Wave is opt-in and the person controls everything.
