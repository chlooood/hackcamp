## Theme
Identify a problem facing a community and build a solution that makes a meaningful difference. The community can be fellow students, a neighbourhood, or people around the world. Any form is fine: app, website, hardware, AI tool, or something else.

## Judging criteria (no weights given, no further detail from judges)
- Problem Research: understanding of the problem, community, and user needs
- Technical Implementation: use and proficiency of the technologies used
- Design: UI/UX, accessibility, ease of use
- Innovation: creativity, originality, impact of the solution
- Pitch: clarity, preparedness, communication of the problem and solution

## Prize rules
- Each team can opt in to ONE superlative prize maximum. Treat every "Best Use of..." and "Best Domain Name" prize as a superlative. Using a sponsor's tech without opting in is allowed.

- Best Use of Vultr (assorted prizes)
