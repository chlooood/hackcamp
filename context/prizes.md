# Prizes (HackCamp 2026)

Sources: the prize list the user pasted (2026-11-08) and https://hackcamp-2026.devpost.com/ (read 2026-11-08). Devpost lists 21 non-cash prizes. It does not say how many opt-ins a team may use.

## Automatic, no opt-in
- Overall Winner: highest-scoring project. Bluetooth speakers, totes, Stanley minis, ElevenLabs subscriptions, automatic admission to nwHacks.
- Beginner Overall Winner: highest-scoring project in the Beginner Stream (no definition of "beginner" found on Devpost). Headphones, keyboards, ElevenLabs, nwHacks admission.
- nwChoice Award: picked by the nwPlus team for creativity, execution or overall experience. 4 Tailscale Premium Plans.

## Open to all, opt in (4 Tailscale Premium Plans each)
- Best Design: outstanding visual design, UX, creativity, execution. Needs a Figma link.
- Community Impact: potential for positive impact on a community. Needs a brief impact statement in Devpost.
- Best Hardware: needs a hardware component. Not for us.

## Beginner only, opt in
- Most Accessible Design: Figma link plus a short description of accessibility considerations.
- Best Problem-Solution Fit: problem statement and solution description in Devpost.
- Open question: does the team count as beginner? Not decided.

## MLH (opt in)
- Best Use of Gemini API (MLH swag kits): incorporate Gemini. We use it for the scan.
- Best Use of Auth0 (wireless headphones). Best Use of Solana, Tiger Data, Vultr, GoDaddy domain. Not planned.
- Best Use of Entire (mini projector): "leverage as many of Entire's core capabilities (Sessions, Graph, and/or Brain) as possible". Devpost does not use the standard opt-in wording. Dev tooling only for us, not a target.

## Sponsor (opt in)
- Freebuff ($200/$125/$75), Databricks, n8n, DeepSpace, Maritime (AI agent), Lovable ($200). None planned.

## What fits us
- Community Impact fits the destigmatization mission and costs only an impact statement. Use the sourced stats and the "surveys, not proof" caveat. Claim potential, not measured impact.
- Best Design fits the design lead's work and needs a Figma link, so someone must put the final screens in Figma before submission.
- Gemini is the scan, so the opt-in costs no extra build.
- If the team is limited to one opt-in, that is a choice between these three. Decide after the organizer answers.
